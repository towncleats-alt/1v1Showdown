Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · 1 ON 1 Showdown Faisalabad — Hosted by Towncleats

1. Information We Collect

Players are enrolled directly by the tournament administrator. When a player is added, we collect: their full name, Gmail address, cell phone number, WhatsApp number, preferred goalkeeper, and any football skills they choose to share. We do not collect national ID numbers, financial card details, or any document scans.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage your tournament enrollment and match fixtures.
  • To send you your match schedule, reminders, and official results via WhatsApp or email.
  • To display your name and tournament statistics on the public leaderboard.
  • To contact you on event day regarding match times or rule clarifications.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in an encrypted PostgreSQL database hosted locally on tournament infrastructure. Passwords are hashed using scrypt (industry-standard, never stored in plain text). We apply rate limiting, input validation, and security headers on all API endpoints. Access to player data is restricted to authorised tournament administrators only.

4. We Never Share Your Data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party, advertiser, or sponsor. Match results and tournament standings displayed publicly contain only your name and game statistics — never your phone number or email address.

5. WhatsApp & Email Communications

By being enrolled in the tournament, you consent to receiving tournament-related WhatsApp messages and emails — such as match reminders, schedule updates, and result notifications. You will not receive spam or marketing messages. These communications are limited to the tournament period (Oct 11, 2026).

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time before the tournament by contacting us on WhatsApp at +92 304 5534884. After the event, anonymised match statistics may be retained for historical records.

7. Contact

For any privacy concerns: towncleats@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 304 5534884.
